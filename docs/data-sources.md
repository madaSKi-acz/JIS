## Attribution

The map must always show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" with a link to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. MapLibre's attribution control does this automatically from the base map style.

## Why not use OSM's own tile server?

`tile.openstreetmap.org` has a [usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) that doesn't allow heavy use by apps. OpenFreeMap is free and made for this.

## Known data gaps in Cambodia

- Intercity bus routes and ferry schedules are incomplete.
- Tourism coverage is good in Phnom Penh, Siem Reap and Kampot, weaker in rural provinces.
- Many places lack `name:km` or `name:en`.

These are great ways to contribute in OpenStreetMap itself.
